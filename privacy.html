Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

BAYTO LLC (“BAYTO,” “we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information you share when you contact us through this website.

Information we collect

When you send a project inquiry, we collect the details you provide in the form, such as your name, work email, organization, decision context and project summary.

How we use information

We use inquiry details only to review your request, reply to you and manage related project conversations. We do not sell your personal information.

How information is sent

Form submissions are delivered through our form provider so we can receive your message by email. Do not include sensitive personal data that is not needed for the inquiry.

Retention

We keep inquiry records only as long as needed to respond and support an active or prospective engagement, unless a longer period is required by law.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact BAYTO LLC through the project inquiry form.
