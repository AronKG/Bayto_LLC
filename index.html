How we work with you

  1. 01

    Define the decision

    Agree on the decision, who will use the findings and what evidence they need.

  2. 02

    Set the scope

    Review available data, define success criteria and agree on methods, deliverables and milestones.

  3. 03

    Review the evidence

    Share early findings with your team and, where relevant, local partners to check assumptions and interpret results.

  4. 04

    Hand over the work

    Deliver the agreed files, document key assumptions and review what the findings mean for your decision.

About BAYTO

BAYTO LLC is a Texas-based consulting firm focused on research design, data analysis, applied AI and application services. We welcome projects from research teams, health organizations and businesses across the United States, Africa and Europe.

  • Based in Texas.
  • Available for international projects.
  • Application services including TMS, POS, card or tag-key systems and web applications.

For cross-border work, we agree with your team on local context, data access, language needs and review responsibilities.
